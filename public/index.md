## Destinations

### Rio de Janeiro
Iconic destination: Christ the Redeemer, Copacabana, Ipanema, Sugarloaf Mountain, Rio Carnival. Best time: April–September.

### São Paulo
Brazil's business and cultural hub: MASP Museum, trade fairs, MICE events, gastronomy. Mild climate year-round.

### Brasília
Brazil's planned capital. UNESCO World Heritage. Oscar Niemeyer architecture. Best time: May–September (dry season).

### Salvador
Brazil's cultural heart: Afro-Brazilian heritage, historic Pelourinho district, beaches and music. Best time: September–March.

### Fortaleza
Tropical beaches, dunes and kite surfing. Best time: July–December.

### Foz do Iguaçu
Iguaçu Falls — one of the world's largest waterfalls. Best time: April–October.

### Vitória
Scenic coastal city, local gastronomy and business hub of Espírito Santo state.

### Maceió
Crystal-clear natural pools, beaches and seafood. Best time: September–March.

### Florianópolis
Island city with 42 beaches, ecotourism and surfing. Best time: December–March.
